Web VPython

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Web VPython (glowscript.org) is a free, open-source site for writing and running 3D physics programs in your browser. It is run by volunteers from the VPython project, and it is not a commercial service. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Signing in

You can run programs without signing in. To save programs, you sign in with your Google account. When you do, Google tells us your email address, your name and your profile picture. We ask for nothing more: no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else in your Google account.

What we store

When you create an account, we store:

  • your email address, to recognize you when you sign in again;
  • the username you choose, which appears in the web address of your programs;
  • the date you joined.

We don't store your name or profile picture. They are kept only in your browser's sign-in cookie for as long as you stay signed in.

For each program you save, we store its name, description and source code, a thumbnail picture of its output, when it was last saved, and the folder it is in, including whether that folder is public or private.

Everything is stored in Google Cloud Datastore, which Google runs for us.

Who can see your programs

  • Public folders: anyone with the link can see and run the programs in them, and can see your username.
  • Private folders: only you can open these programs in the editor. But if you use "Share or export this program" to embed a private program in a web page, anyone who visits that page can run it.
  • Site administrators: the two administrators can open private programs, but only to recover programs for a user who can no longer sign in, or to fix problems.

Your email address is never shown to other users.

Cookies

The site uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It is needed for the site to work. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Server logs

Like any web server, ours (Google App Engine) keeps routine request logs, including IP addresses and browser types. We use them only to keep the site running and to fix problems, and they are deleted after 30 days.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't track you with analytics.
  • We don't use your information for anything except running the site.
  • We don't share your information with anyone except Google, which hosts the site.
  • We use the email address Google gives us only to identify your account. We don't use it to email you.

Deleting your programs or your account

You can delete your own programs and folders at any time from your program list. To delete your whole account and everything in it, email stevespicklemire@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with.

Children

Web VPython is a general-audience site for anyone learning or teaching physics and programming. It is not aimed at any particular age group. We don't ask for your age, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 except as described here, for example when a school uses the site with its students. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without the consent of a parent or school, email stevespicklemire@gmail.com and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about the site: the glowscript-users forum. Private privacy requests: stevespicklemire@gmail.com.
